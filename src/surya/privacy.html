Surya Saathi privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026.

Surya Saathi is a WhatsApp assistant that reads an electricity bill and estimates whether rooftop solar under the PM Surya Ghar scheme is worth it for a household. It is a student project built for the WeMakeDevs × AWS Bharat Builds hackathon.

What we read from your bill

Only the fields needed for the estimate: electricity provider, tariff category, sanctioned load, units used per month, the current month's charges and the pincode. The model reading the bill is never asked for your name, address, phone number or consumer number.

What we keep

  • Your WhatsApp number, chosen language, any location you share, and a short summary of your last estimate (recommended size and verdict). This is deleted automatically 90 days after your last message.
  • WhatsApp message IDs, to avoid answering the same message twice. These are deleted after 2 days.

The bill photo or PDF itself is not stored. It is processed in memory and discarded.

Comparisons and solar passport

A quote comparison keeps a random request ID, state, system size, optional PIN code, the quotes and your choice for up to 30 days. A WhatsApp comparison is linked to your WhatsApp account so you can retrieve or delete it. During the hackathon the vendors are fictional demo vendors: choosing one contacts no one and shares nothing. Use Delete comparison to remove a website comparison.

The solar passport is saved only when you choose Save on this device, in this browser's local storage. Use Clear saved passport to remove it. An exported copy stays wherever you save it and must be deleted there separately. Quote previews, offer checks, budgets and manual generation/bill reviews are processed to return results and are not retained by these tools on the server.

Who processes it

Messages travel through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform or Twilio's demo sandbox. The bill is read by a model on Amazon Bedrock, and the cloud backend runs on Amazon Web Services in the Mumbai region. Sunlight data comes from the EU's PVGIS service and NASA POWER, which receive only a rounded location, never your bill. We do not sell your data or share it with solar vendors.

Deleting your data

Send DELETE MY DATA to the Surya Saathi WhatsApp number and everything stored about you is removed immediately. You can also email piyushkumar07999@gmail.com.

Estimates, not advice

Savings and payback figures are estimates based on published tariffs, scheme rules and sunlight data. Always compare quotes from more than one registered vendor before deciding.
